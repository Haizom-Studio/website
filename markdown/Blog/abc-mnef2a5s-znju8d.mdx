Launching a **web application** is rarely “flip a switch.” Production traffic exposes **misconfiguration**, **missing headers**, **leaky secrets**, and **observability** gaps that local development never stressed. This checklist is organized for teams shipping **SSR**, **SPA**, or **hybrid** stacks—adapt sections to your hosting (Vercel, AWS, Cloudflare, self-hosted).

## HTTPS and certificates

- **TLS** terminates correctly on all public hostnames; no mixed **HTTP** assets on **HTTPS** pages.
- **Redirects** consolidate to one canonical host (pick **www** or **apex**—not both serving duplicate 200s).
- **HSTS** (`Strict-Transport-Security`) once you are confident redirects are correct—avoid locking users to broken HTTPS during migration.

Test from **outside** your office network; corporate proxies hide problems.

## Secrets and configuration

- **API keys**, **database URLs**, and **signing keys** live in **environment** or a **secrets manager**—never committed to git.
- **Client-exposed** keys (e.g., public analytics IDs) are **expected** to be public; anything else does not belong in the browser bundle.
- **Rotate** credentials if they ever leaked in a ticket, screenshot, or build log.

Document **required** env vars in an internal runbook so deploys are reproducible.

## Security headers (baseline)

Exact directives depend on your **CSP** strategy, but review:

- **`Content-Security-Policy`** — start strict in staging; loosen only with reason.
- **`X-Frame-Options`** or **`frame-ancestors`** — clickjacking protection.
- **`Referrer-Policy`** — balance privacy and debugging needs.
- **`Permissions-Policy`** — disable unused browser features.

Framework defaults help—**verify** they are not disabled in production.

## Application-level controls

- **Authentication** and **authorization** enforced **server-side** on every sensitive route and API.
- **CSRF** protection for cookie-based sessions where applicable.
- **Rate limiting** on login, password reset, and expensive APIs.
- **File uploads** validated for type, size, and scanned if user-facing.

## SEO and indexing hygiene

- **Unique** `<title>` and meta **description** per public route.
- **`robots.txt`** and **`sitemap.xml`** for sites that want full indexing; **`noindex`** on **staging** and **preview** deployments.
- **Canonical** URLs for duplicate paths (tracking params, A/B tests).

Accidental indexing of **staging** is a common source of duplicate-content headaches.

## Observability — logs, metrics, traces

- **Structured logging** from servers (JSON lines) with **request IDs**.
- **Error tracking** (Sentry or similar) on **client** and **server** with **PII** scrubbing.
- **Uptime** checks on **critical** endpoints from multiple regions.
- **Alerts** that wake someone—**not** only email into a void.

Define **SLOs** for availability and error rate before launch day heroics.

## Performance and caching

- **CDN** caching rules for static assets; **`Cache-Control`** semantics correct for **HTML** vs **immutable** assets.
- **Database** connection pools sized for expected concurrency—**load test** a realistic scenario.

## Backups and recovery

- **Database backups** automated, **restored** on a schedule (untested backups are fiction).
- **Runbook** for rollback: previous container image, **migration** strategy, feature flags.

## Legal and privacy

- **Privacy policy** and **cookie** notices match actual trackers.
- **Data processing** agreements if you handle EU or regulated data.

## Launch day — sensible sequencing

1. **Freeze** non-critical changes; ship **fixes** only.
2. **Smoke test** critical paths in production **before** announcing.
3. **Monitor** dashboards for the first **hours** actively—not only automated pings.

## Summary

Production readiness is **discipline**: transport security, secret hygiene, headers, observability, and indexing rules—**verified**, not assumed. Run this checklist early enough to **fix** issues without a fire drill.
