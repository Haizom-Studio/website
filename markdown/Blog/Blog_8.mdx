**Technical SEO** is the work that makes valuable pages **discoverable** and **consistent** for search engines: internal links, canonical URLs, honest metadata, and rendering that does not hide primary content behind fragile client-only paths. Marketing invests in messaging; **web development** must ensure crawlers can fetch, render, and attribute signals to the URLs you intend. When that breaks, teams argue about “rankings” while the real issue is **coverage** and **duplicate** confusion.

## Crawling: help bots spend budget on pages that matter

Orphan pages, infinite filter URLs, and JS-gated content waste crawl budget and fragment signals. Maintain hierarchy with navigation and contextual links; verify critical content in HTML or reliably rendered output; use **robots.txt** to block useless paths—not to hide content you want indexed.

## Canonicalization: duplicates dilute signals

HTTP vs HTTPS, www vs apex, tracking parameters, and sort variants can all create “the same” content at multiple URLs. **Canonical** tags help when duplicates must exist; **redirects** and consistent internal linking are better. Technical debt here shows up as wrong snippets in SERPs and split link equity.

## Titles and descriptions: clarity beats keyword stuffing

Unique titles that front-load the topic improve relevance and click-through. Meta descriptions influence **CTR**; they do not replace good content. Templates help at scale—watch for thin duplicates across thousands of URLs.

## Structured data—truth on the page only

**JSON-LD** for Article, Product, or FAQ can unlock rich results when eligible. Mark up what users actually see; validate with search tools. Misleading schema risks manual actions.

## Performance and mobile-first indexing

Slow, unstable mobile experiences limit how thoroughly sites are crawled and how users engage. Align template work with **Core Web Vitals** on URLs that carry organic traffic—**web development** choices (SSR, data loading, asset strategy) are inseparable from technical SEO outcomes.

## Internationalization and staging

Use **hreflang** thoughtfully for language/region variants; avoid IP redirects that hide crawlable alternates. Block staging hosts from indexing so preview content does not compete with production.

## Measurement

Search Console for coverage; server logs where policy allows; analytics on landing pages that actually earn visits. **Dashboards** for SEO should tie to URLs and templates—not only sitewide averages.
## Summary

Technical SEO is **hygiene plus architecture**: URLs users can trust, metadata that matches reality, crawlable rendering, and fast delivery. Get that right, and content and backlinks can do their job.
